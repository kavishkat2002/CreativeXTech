## 1. The Core Philosophy of AEO & GEO

Unlike traditional keyword-density search optimization, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) rely on **information gain, direct factual extraction, unambiguous entity disambiguation, and verified structural authority**.

Large Language Models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines ingest web documents and score passages based on:

1. **Passage Extractability (Answer-First Format):** Can the model quote or summarize a definitive answer in the first 1–2 sentences without filler or ambiguous phrasing?
2. **Entity Grounding & Disambiguation:** Does the document unambiguously identify the entity as **Creativex Technology (Pvt) Ltd**, headquartered at *166 / B Perera Mawatha, Rajagiriya, Colombo, Sri Lanka*, distinct from overseas platforms such as `creativex.com`?
3. **Structured Authority (JSON-LD):** Are company facts supported by Schema.org graphs (`Organization`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `JobPosting`, `BreadcrumbList`) anchored to `https://www.creativextechnology.com/#organization`?
4. **Machine-Readable Summaries:** Does the domain provide structured plain text documentation via `/llms.txt` and semantic HTML tags (`<article>`, `<section>`, `<h1>`-`<h3>`)?
